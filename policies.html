I · Bespoke Policy

Made-to-Order & Returns

Because each garment from Revikaraj Rathore India is customized to individual measurements and handcrafted over hundreds of artisan hours, all bespoke and made-to-order pieces are final sale and non-returnable.

Should any piece not drape to your exact satisfaction upon delivery, our master cutters and embroidery artisans provide dedicated alteration and fitting support to achieve the perfect silhouette.

Book a bespoke consultation
II · Fitting Support

Alterations & Trial Fittings

For bridal poshak commissions and bridal lehengas, we offer structured trial fittings in our atelier or via virtual measurement consultations with our head pattern maker.

We provide complimentary initial alterations within 14 days of delivery. Clients outside India may coordinate fitting adjustments with our dedicated bridal concierge team.

III · Logistics

Insured Worldwide Delivery

All Revikaraj Rathore India orders are dispatched in archival garment boxes with tamper-evident seals and full transit insurance via premier carriers (DHL Express, FedEx, and BlueDart).

Timelines:

• Ready to Ship Pieces: Dispatched within 3–5 business days.
• Made to Order Saree Edits: 4–6 weeks from measurement confirmation.
• Bridal Poshaks & Royal Lehengas: 8–12 weeks depending on zardozi density.

International recipients are responsible for any destination customs duties or import taxes assessed by their local authorities.

IV · Discretion

Privacy & Client Confidentiality

Revikaraj Rathore India upholds the highest standards of discretion for our royal, celebrity, and discerning private clientele. Personal contact information, bridal measurements, private consultations, and custom motif designs are held under strict confidentiality.

We never sell or disclose client details to third parties. Information collected during consultations or newsletter subscriptions is utilized exclusively to manage your orders and share private salon invitations.

V · Preservation

Textile & Hand-Embroidery Care

Heritage handwork requires gentle, thoughtful preservation. We advise the following atelier care standards:

• Dry Clean Only: Always entrust pieces to specialized dry cleaners experienced in raw silk and real metal thread embroidery.
• Muslin Wrap: Store sarees and poshaks wrapped in unbleached pure cotton or muslin fabric. Never store in plastic pouches.
• Refolding: Periodically refold garments along fresh crease lines every season to prevent permanent tension on zardozi threads.
• Perfumes & Moisture: Apply fragrances, mists, and cosmetics prior to donning hand-embroidered drapes to avoid tarnishing beaten metal wires.
