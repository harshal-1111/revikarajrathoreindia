I · Founders

A house of hand, thread & heirloom

The founders set out to keep these crafts in everyday wear, pairing archival motifs with cuts made for the way women dress today. Each collection returns to the archive and carries something new out of it.

Book a bespoke consultation
II · Craftmanship

Made on the frame, by hand

Every piece begins at a wooden frame, where our artisans work thread by thread in techniques passed down through their families. Zardozi, dabka and aari are laid by hand, and some drapes take a full season to finish.
